The more channels you run, the less you can see

A back office per channel, a set of rules per channel, someone responsible for each. None of it reconciles in the middle.

  • One price change means opening several back offices and doing it several times. Miss one and it costs you.
  • Inventory is counted per channel, so you find out you oversold after it happens.
  • Adding a channel starts with whether you have the people.
  • Something sells on a marketplace and you see an order, not a person.
