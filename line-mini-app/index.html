LINE Mini App for E-commerce Businesses

A LINE Mini App is an application that runs inside LINE. Customers don't download anything and don't sign up. They tap in already logged in, and they can browse products, answer a survey, and pay.

For an e-commerce brand, the point isn't one more channel. It's that the entire purchase can finish inside LINE, without sending the customer out to a website.

LINE Mini App vs LIFF

Both render web pages inside LINE, and the experience is not close. This is the thing worth understanding before you choose.

LIFFLINE Mini App
How it feelsA mobile website opened inside LINEA lightweight app living inside LINE
LoginRedirects to log in, so the flow breaks easilyAutomatic
Back buttonHard to predictNavigation managed by LINE
PaymentLINE Pay redirects bounce back and forthPayment completes on the same screen
MessagingReach depends on friend statusService messages regardless of friend status
Entry pointThrough a linkA permanent home in LINE's Wallet tab
SuitsCold ad traffic, guest checkoutA full storefront with an established friend base

Short version: if you're catching cold ad traffic and the job is to get someone through checkout fast, LIFF is enough. If you're running a storefront for the long term and you already have friends on LINE, the experience gap shows.

Why this fits Taiwan e-commerce

People in Taiwan are already in LINE all day. The real value is subtraction: no download, no signup, no password to remember, no re-entering an address.

Every step in an e-commerce flow loses people. A Mini App removes the steps that lose the most.

What e-commerce brands use it for

Guest checkout Cold traffic from ads can buy without registering as a member first.

Product discovery A survey or quiz points customers to the right product instead of leaving them to dig through a category page.

Customer data Interactions become tags tied to a LINE identity, so next time you know who to talk to.

Offline into online Stores, pop-ups, an NFC or QR tag on the packaging. One tap and they're in LINE.

After the sale Order status, shipping updates, and reorder reminders all live in the same conversation.

What building one actually involves

The idea is simple. The work is not. Three things take the time.

Review LINE runs an approval process for Mini Apps, with specs and submission requirements to follow.

Engineering Login, payments, service messages, and member binding each have their own API. They have to be wired together before the flow feels right.

Operations Once it's live, someone maintains the campaigns, the products, and the messaging. Launch isn't the finish line.

Common questions

How is a Mini App different from a LINE Official Account? The Official Account is where you talk to customers. The Mini App is where they do things. One handles the conversation, the other handles the flow. They work together.

Do customers have to add us as a friend first? No. Service messages in a Mini App don't depend on friend status, so someone can buy first and become a friend later.

We already have a website and sell on marketplaces. Do we need this? It depends where your traffic comes from. If your customers arrive through LINE, social, or in person, letting them finish in LINE beats routing them to your site. Your website doesn't go away. These are different entry points.

Can we build it ourselves? Yes. What to weigh is the cost of approvals, integration, and ongoing maintenance, plus how soon your engineering team could actually start.
