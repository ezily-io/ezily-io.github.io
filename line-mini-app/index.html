LINE Mini App vs LIFF

Both render web pages inside LINE, and the experience is not close. This is the thing worth understanding before you choose.

LIFF

  • Feels like a mobile website opened inside LINE
  • Redirects to log in, so the flow breaks easily
  • Back button is hard to predict
  • LINE Pay redirects bounce back and forth
  • Reach depends on friend status
  • Accessed through a link
  • Suits cold ad traffic and guest checkout

LINE Mini App

  • Feels like a lightweight app living inside LINE
  • Automatic login
  • Navigation managed by LINE
  • Payment completes on the same screen
  • Service messages regardless of friend status
  • A permanent home in LINE's Wallet tab
  • Suits a full storefront with an established friend base

Why this fits Taiwan e-commerce

People in Taiwan are already in LINE all day. The real value is subtraction: no download, no signup, no password to remember, no address to re-enter.

Every step in an e-commerce flow loses people. A Mini App removes the steps that lose the most.

What e-commerce brands use it for

From first-time buyers to repeat customers, online to offline, every interaction becomes a lead you can act on next time.

Guest checkout

Cold traffic from ads can buy without registering first.

Product discovery

A survey or quiz points customers to the right product instead of leaving them to dig through a category page.

Customer data

Interactions become tags tied to a LINE identity, so next time you know who to talk to.

Offline into online

Stores, pop-ups, an NFC or QR tag on the packaging. One tap and they're in LINE.

After the sale

Order status, shipping updates, and reorder reminders all live in the same conversation.

What building one actually involves

The idea is simple. The work is not. Three things take the time.

Review

LINE runs an approval process for Mini Apps, with specs and submission requirements to follow.

Engineering

Login, payments, service messages, and member binding each have their own API. They have to be wired together before the flow feels right.

Operations

Once it's live, someone maintains the campaigns, the products, and the messaging. Launch is not the finish line.

Common questions

How is a Mini App different from a LINE Official Account?
The Official Account is where you talk to customers. The Mini App is where they do things. One handles the conversation, the other handles the flow. They work together.
Do customers have to add us as a friend first?
No. Service messages in a Mini App don't depend on friend status, so someone can buy first and become a friend later.
We already have a website and sell on marketplaces. Do we need this?
It depends where your traffic comes from. If your customers arrive through LINE, social, or in person, letting them finish in LINE beats routing them to your site. Your website doesn't go away. These are different entry points.
Can we build it ourselves?
Yes. What to weigh is the cost of approvals, integration, and ongoing maintenance, plus how soon your engineering team could actually start.
