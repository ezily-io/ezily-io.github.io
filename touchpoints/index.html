Match the next step
to each customer's moment

Some start with a quiz or a scan. Others go straight to the buying page for a group buy or VIP event.

  1. Learn preferences

    Customers answer a few questions, or scan the QR code on the packaging to collect points.

  2. Recommend

    Based on what they chose, invite them to related products or activities.

  3. Complete the purchase

    The product and the activity details sit together, so they order once they've chosen.

  4. Reach out again

    Note what they bought, and tell them next time something fits.
