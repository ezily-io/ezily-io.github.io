Full Stack Engineer

Build features end to end and stay responsible for what you ship.

Apply Now
In this role
    Apply Now

    Ezily is a team of around twenty building the AI-native commerce layer for e-commerce in Taiwan.

    We build three connected layers. Touchpoints creates customer experiences, including inside LINE. Checkout completes the transaction. Orchestrate manages products, inventory, pricing, and orders in one place and connects them to Momo, Shopee, and other channels.

    The role

    You build features end to end and stay responsible for what you ship.

    We are looking for a "Product-Minded Engineer." This means you care as much about the why as the how. You will own the full lifecycle of your services, from whiteboarding a new API contract to ensuring its performance in production. While there is a path to staff-level leadership, your immediate impact will be measured by your ability to ship elegant code and level up the engineers around you.

    Key Responsibilities:

    1. Technical Leadership: Lead the design and delivery of complex full-stack features, taking ownership of technical decisions within your domains.
    2. System Design: Contribute to the evolution of scalable, maintainable architectures and ensure solutions balance business needs with long-term system health.
    3. Execution: Remain hands-on in building and shipping high-quality software while driving momentum and removing technical roadblocks.
    4. Mentorship: Support the growth of other engineers through code reviews, design discussions, and technical guidance.
    5. Collaboration: Work closely with product and engineering partners to translate business goals into pragmatic technical solutions.
    6. Operational Ownership: Take responsibility for services you build, from development through production, following a “you build it, you run it” mindset.

    Our Core Stack:

    1. Frontend: React, Next.js, TypeScript
    2. Backend: Node.js, TypeScript
    3. Data: PostgreSQL
    4. Infrastructure: AWS, Docker, Terraform
    5. Architecture: Headless, API-first, multi-product systems

    Qualifications & Experience:

    1. Experience: 7+ years of professional software development experience, with prior experience leading or mentoring other engineers.
    2. Full-Stack Expertise: Strong hands-on experience across backend and frontend development in modern web applications.
    3. System Design: Proven ability to design and evolve distributed systems, and to make informed trade-offs around scalability, consistency, and reliability.
    4. Ownership Mentality: Comfortable owning systems end-to-end and being accountable for production outcomes.
    5. Communication: Strong English communication skills; able to explain technical decisions clearly to both engineers and non-technical stakeholders.
    6. Mindset: Strong opinions, weakly held. Pragmatic, honest, and focused on shipping value.

    Nice to Have:

    1. Polyglot Roots: Experience in Ruby, Go, or Python (we value the ability to choose the right tool for the job).
    2. Domain Expertise: Prior experience in e-commerce, payments, or logistics.
    3. Scale: Experience handling high-throughput APIs or complex 3rd-party integrations.
    4. Open-source contributions, technical writing, or community involvement.
    5. Experience working in fast-moving startup or scale-up environments..
