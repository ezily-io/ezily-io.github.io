The problem

The problem is genuinely hard

LINE's review process, product data across channels, real-time inventory. None of it gets solved by building one more interface.

Brands should own their customers

Most e-commerce tooling is built around a single sale. Bring in traffic, sell, finish, then pay for the traffic again.

We want to replace that loop. Every interaction stays with the brand and becomes their own understanding of their customers, instead of renting reach from a platform forever.

We are based in Taiwan. We work with brands selling here, and with brands trying to get here.
