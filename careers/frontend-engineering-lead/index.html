Lead Frontend Engineer

Decide the frontend architecture. Write the code. Shape the experience.

Apply Now
In this role
    Apply Now

    Ezily is a team of around twenty building the AI-native commerce layer for e-commerce in Taiwan.

    We build three connected layers. Touchpoints creates customer experiences, including inside LINE. Checkout completes the transaction. Orchestrate manages products, inventory, pricing, and orders in one place and connects them to Momo, Shopee, and other channels.

    The role

    You decide the frontend architecture and write the code. The products include AI features, streaming responses among them.

    You set the technical direction and lead the core architecture behind the web products, mentor the engineers, and work across teams. The role asks you to balance hands-on development, team guidance, and strategic calls, and all three are part of it.

    Key Responsibilities

    1. Architecture & Problem Solving: Architect scalable frontend systems, component structures, and application patterns while solving technical problems of moderate-to-high scope and complexity.
    2. AI-Accelerated Delivery: Use AI-assisted tools to quickly turn product intent, requirements, or design concepts into working UI drafts, then review, refactor, and shape the output into production-ready implementation.
    3. AI-Native Product Experience: Build frontend experiences for AI-enabled products, including streaming responses, chat or agentic interfaces, latency and loading states, fallback behavior, and graceful handling of imperfect model output.
    4. Code Quality & Frontend Standards: Deliver high-quality, maintainable code while improving frontend foundations such as component libraries, design tokens, accessibility, QA practices, and review standards.
    5. Product & Design Collaboration: Work closely with Product Management, UX, and other stakeholders to iterate on features, close the gap between design and shipped product, and improve the overall Ezily product experience.
    6. Team Leadership: Mentor and provide guidance to frontend engineers, helping them grow while raising the team’s technical quality and development practices.

    Qualification

    1. Frontend Mastery: High proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML, CSS, React or other modern frontend frameworks, performance, accessibility, and modern browser concepts.
    2. Architectural Expertise: Extensive experience in technical decision-making, architectural planning, and implementing engineering best practices and coding standards.
    3. AI Tooling & Review Judgment: Practical experience using AI-assisted development tools to support prototyping, implementation, refactoring, debugging, or design-to-code workflows, with the judgment to know when to trust, refactor, or rewrite generated output.
    4. Product & Interface Judgment: Strong product sense and visual judgment, with the ability to distinguish between UI that merely works and UI that feels clear, polished, usable, and appropriate for the product.
    5. Ownership & Stakeholder Management: A track record of owning outcomes, prioritizing effectively, delegating tasks when needed, and managing relationships with cross-functional stakeholders.
    6. Communication Skills: Excellent written and verbal English skills, with the ability to clearly communicate technical or organizational problems and propose thorough solutions.
    7. Leadership & Mentorship: A proven track record of leading engineering initiatives, mentoring developers, and driving technical direction or quality improvements.
    8. Technical Tools & Workflow: Proficiency with Git in a collaborative workplace environment and experience working within a structured development workflow.
    9. Mindset & Adaptability: A positive, solution-oriented mindset with the comfort to thrive in a dynamic, quick-moving environment and a desire for long-term growth within the organization.

    Nice to Have

    1. Generative UI & AI Integration: Experience building agentic features, AI-driven workflows, prompt-to-UI generators, dynamic interfaces integrated with LLMs, or AI-assisted product creation tools.
    2. Design Systems & Framework Development: Experience building component libraries, design tokens, frontend platform standards, custom frontend frameworks, or contributing significantly to established open-source libraries.
    3. AI-Native UX Patterns: Experience designing or implementing streaming UX, chat interfaces, agent workflows, feedback capture, human-in-the-loop review flows, or model-output validation patterns.
    4. Startup Growth: Experience working in a high-performance environment, ideally within a fast-growing tech startup.
    5. Agile Leadership & Tech Advocacy: Experience leading Agile or Lean development practices, blog writing, public speaking, or active knowledge sharing within the engineering community.
