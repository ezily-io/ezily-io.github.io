We think customers should belong to the brand

Most e-commerce tooling is built around a single sale. Bring in traffic, sell, finish, then pay for the traffic again.

We build for the other thing. Every interaction becomes the brand's own understanding of their customers, and that decides who to talk to next, and what to offer them.

We are based in Taiwan. We work with brands selling here, and with brands trying to get here.
