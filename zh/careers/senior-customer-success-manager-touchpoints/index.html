(Senior) Customer Success Manager (Touchpoints)

從導入、上線到成效回顧，品牌由你帶。

    立即應徵

    職缺說明以英文提供。

    Ezily is a team of around twenty building the AI-native commerce layer for e-commerce in Taiwan.

    We build three connected layers. Touchpoints creates customer experiences, including inside LINE. Checkout completes the transaction. Orchestrate manages products, inventory, pricing, and orders in one place and connects them to Momo, Shopee, and other channels.

    The role

    You take brands from their first conversation about Touchpoints to a live use case in LINE, then sit down with them over the results and agree what comes next.

    We're looking for a hands-on Customer Success Manager to help brands turn business goals into measurable experiences using Ezily Touchpoints and LINE Mini Apps. You'll guide customers through onboarding, implementation, launch, and performance review, while building repeatable ways to help them use the product effectively.

    Ezily Touchpoints is still validating its most repeatable use cases and product-market fit. You will work with customers and our GTM, Product, Engineering, and Analytics teams to test assumptions, solve launch blockers, and turn real usage and feedback into product learning. The role calls for someone who has worked in an early-stage startup and is comfortable making progress when processes and product capabilities are still evolving.

    Key Responsibilities:

    1. Own onboarding and activation for assigned brands. Understand each customer's goals, stakeholders, existing LINE and commerce setup, and success criteria; train users and develop a clear plan for their first live Touchpoints use case.
    2. Translate customer needs into testable use cases and interaction flows. Scope the customer journey, define content and data requirements, coordinate setup and testing, and work with the brand and internal teams toward launch.
    3. Monitor activation and campaign performance using dashboards, spreadsheets, and available analytics tools. Review exposure, engagement, completion, and conversion funnels; identify drop-offs and recommend improvements based on evidence.
    4. Lead customer performance reviews. Explain results in business terms, acknowledge data limitations, capture what worked and what did not, and agree on a practical next step for product use or validation.
    5. Coordinate with BD, Product, Engineering, and Analytics to address launch blockers, document bugs and feature requests, clarify ownership and priorities, and communicate progress and workable alternatives to customers.
    6. Gather and synthesize customer feedback, usage patterns, and recurring implementation friction. Help the team distinguish individual requests from repeatable needs that may inform product priorities, customer playbooks, and future use-case design.
    7. Prepare concise performance reviews and presentations for customer stakeholders and internal leaders, with evidence, recommendations, owners, and next actions.
    8. Agree with each brand on responsibilities for campaign approvals, LINE broadcasts, commerce or membership operations, customer service, and fulfillment; coordinate the Ezily work within that plan.

    Qualifications:

    1. Approximately 3 to 5 years of relevant experience in B2B SaaS customer success, implementation, solutions consulting, or account management, including independently leading customer projects from discovery through launch and review.
    2. Experience working in an early-stage startup where product-market fit, use cases, and operating processes were still evolving; able to test assumptions and make sound decisions with incomplete information.
    3. Experience supporting e-commerce, retail, beauty, lifestyle, or other consumer brands, and an understanding of customer acquisition, membership, and online-to-offline journeys.
    4. Ability to interpret campaign or product usage data, investigate funnel drop-offs, work confidently with spreadsheets and dashboards, and explain findings as practical recommendations.
    5. Proven ability to influence stakeholders and build strong relationships across multiple organizational layers.
    6. Strong written and verbal communication, including the ability to turn ambiguous customer requests or technical limitations into clear plans, decisions, and updates.
    7. Experience engaging with senior executives and decision-makers, with sound business judgment and strategic insight.
    8. Ability to create reusable customer-facing and internal materials, such as onboarding guides, FAQs, use-case playbooks, and success case documentation.

    Preferred Qualifications:

    1. Experience with LINE Official Accounts, LINE Mini Apps, CRM or membership journeys, and e-commerce platforms or integrations.
    2. Professional English communication for cross-functional work; Japanese language ability is an advantage.
    3. Experience with PostHog or similar product analytics tools; SQL skills are a plus.
