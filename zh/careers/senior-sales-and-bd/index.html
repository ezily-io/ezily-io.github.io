    立即應徵

    職缺說明以英文提供。

    Ezily is a team of around twenty building the AI-native commerce layer for e-commerce in Taiwan.

    We build three connected layers. Touchpoints creates customer experiences, including inside LINE. Checkout completes the transaction. Orchestrate manages products, inventory, pricing, and orders in one place and connects them to Momo, Shopee, and other channels.

    The role

    You own the commercial pipeline end to end and carry an individual revenue target. You source your own deals rather than working an inbound queue, and you run each one from the first conversation through contract to a clean handoff.

    The playbook exists. Part of the job is finding where it breaks in front of a real customer and fixing it.

    Key Responsibilities:

    1. Own the end-to-end commercial pipeline and individual revenue target.
      1. Own the individual revenue targets, which are set collaboratively with management.
      2. Independently generate leads through targeted outbound, referrals, partnerships, industry relationships, events, and founder-supported introductions, with direct accountability for new-logo revenue, deal progression, and quota attainment.
      3. Qualify and prioritize opportunities on customer pain, ICP fit, urgency, buying readiness, strategic value, and adoption likelihood.
      4. Run the full cycle: discovery, proposal, negotiation, close, and structured handoff to Customer Success.
      5. Maintain accurate CRM data, forecasts, deal risks, and next steps, and identify and fix bottlenecks in pipeline quality, conversion, and velocity.
    2. Own deal-level negotiation and contracting, and supply pricing evidence.
      1. Negotiate multi-module subscription pricing and contract terms with enterprise stakeholders, within company pricing guardrails: discount policy, approval thresholds, and standard contract terms, which the CEO holds until the Head of BD joins.
      2. Escalate exceptions per the threshold policy rather than custom-pricing each deal.
      3. Own contract negotiation within pre-approved standard terms and the approved fallback position.
      4. Escalate any non-standard commercial structure to the CEO, and to the Head of BD later: rev-share, multi-year commitments, pilot conversion terms, exclusivity. Feed recurring redline patterns back so standard terms can be tightened.
      5. Log every negotiation as a willingness-to-pay data point and supply it to the pricing owner. That evidence is what pricing architecture is built from.
    3. Own pipeline-level partnerships.
      1. Develop pipeline-related partnerships that generate referrals, co-selling opportunities, and partner-sourced pipeline.
      2. Represent Ezily across relevant ecosystems, including LINE, Shopify, Shopee, and other commerce platforms, with a focus on converting relationships into commercial outcomes.
      3. Escalate any partnership involving product or roadmap commitments, exclusivity, revenue-share that changes the business model, market entry, or board-visible commitments. These sit with the CEO now, and with the Head of BD later.
    4. Own commercial playbook execution.
      1. Adopt and consistently run the existing playbook (ICP definitions, qualification criteria, pipeline stages, discovery process, CRM standards, proposals, and handoffs) and identify where it breaks in real customer situations.
      2. Iterate on pitch material and test the value proposition collaboratively with the Product team, and sharpen objection handling and FAQ to raise close rate.
      3. Document learnings and successful practices so future commercial hires ramp faster and reproduce strong outcomes.
      4. Support the onboarding and coaching of junior BD hires and help improve the team's operating discipline.

    Success Measures:

    1. Hitting revenue target.
    2. Bringing on quality customers.
    3. Contribution to playbook quality and team scalability.

    Required Qualifications:

    1. 8+ years B2B SaaS or tech sales experience, ideally in Taiwan (Japan a plus).
    2. Proven ability to sell into both enterprise accounts (multi-stakeholder, longer cycle) and SME or smaller brands (faster, higher-velocity, lower ACV), not just one or the other.
    3. Track record of self-sourcing pipeline, not just working inbound leads.
    4. Pricing and negotiation fluency across a wide range of deal sizes, with comfort working directly with legal on contracts without heavy support.
    5. Comfortable running discovery-led, hypothesis-driven sales conversations and adapting pitch and negotiation style to buyer sophistication and deal size.
    6. Consistent track record of hitting or exceeding quota.
    7. Strong organizational discipline: CRM hygiene, forecasting, structured follow-up, and documentation habits.
    8. Comfortable operating in a 0-to-1 startup environment, executing against revenue goals while iterating on the GTM playbook, and willing to co-own experiments with Product rather than run a parallel agenda.
    9. Genuine ambition to grow into a leadership role. This is not intended to be a long-term individual-contributor-only seat.
    10. Direct experience selling e-commerce, marketplace, or commerce infrastructure, and fluency in how brands run online channels and fulfillment.
    11. Proven track record selling to brand-side buyers in e-commerce, marketing, or retail leadership, not just IT or technical buyers.

    Preferred Qualifications:

    1. Experience with usage or automation-based products (workflow, integration, or automation platforms).
    2. Mandarin and English fluency; Japanese a plus.
    3. Prior experience selling both an enterprise platform tier and a lightweight SME tier of the same product.
    4. Prior experience closing large Japanese deals.
    5. Prior experience mentoring, onboarding, or informally leading peers, even without a formal management title.
    6. Credibility in the e-commerce ecosystem: long-standing, actively maintained relationships across brands, retailers, marketplaces, and channel or agency partners.
